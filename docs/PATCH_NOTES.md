# EPOCH — Beyond the Signal

**Released September 14, 2026**

**Version 1.0.0 · Build 94e88c74bcb0**

[Play EPOCH](https://epoch.jaqstudios.com/)

Follow Voss beyond the original five missions, trace a missing convoy, and uncover the voices behind the Signal. This release expands the campaign, gives each destination its own identity, and brings new choices to combat and ship preparation.

## Highlights

- **50 story levels across ten chapters**, followed by an endless frontier.
- **Distinct environments and enemy fleets**, with five guardian attack styles.
- **Six-second Supercharge**, clearer weapon progression, and fewer upgrade drops.
- **Training Wheels** makes enemy guides optional and starts off by default.

## Campaign and encounters

- The Signal Map now connects the opening five levels to a larger campaign. Mission briefings and recovered transmissions develop the search for the Signal throughout all 50 authored levels.
- Completing the story preserves a checkpoint for **level 51**. Endless levels continue with increasing durability and varied formations, guardians and scenery.
- Each level contains **8–10 waves**, including its final guardian encounter. A new launch randomly selects **0–2 asteroid waves** among the middle encounters.
- Destructible asteroids cross from the top and both sides at varied angles, including upward passes. Surviving asteroids return along new paths.
- Enemy ships that leave the playfield circle back through hook, slalom or crossing patterns. **Every enemy must be destroyed before the next wave begins.**

## Ship preparation and visual identity

- A compact main menu places **Edit Ship** beside the selected airframe, with clearer access to Continue, Signal Map, Archive and Settings.
- Ship parts and Manta assembly progress are visible in the menu, hangar and flight HUD. Recover **12 parts** to unlock Manta; each ship keeps its own handling and reactor settings.
- All 50 story levels have distinct palettes, scenery arrangements and enemy fleets. Environments range from orbital planets and wreck fields to ice, suspended oceans, rifts and beacon chains.
- Enemy and guardian silhouettes vary by level while retaining recognizable combat roles. Player ships, weapon types and hostile roles use different projectile shapes.

## Combat and guardians

- First-wave enemies now take **4–5 basic Strelka pulse hits each**, using a balanced reactor without upgrades or temporary buffs. This target accounts for pulse weaknesses; other weapons and reactor choices retain their damage differences.
- Projectile weaknesses grant **1.6× damage**: pulse against weavers, scatter against straight drones, and lance against shooters and asteroids. Guardians have their own weaknesses, listed in the hangar field guide.
- Fired projectiles remain active after their source is destroyed and across wave transitions. After the final guardian falls, its remaining shots and mine fragments must clear before the level ends.
- **Warden:** moving projectile gates. **Twins:** curved scissor crossings. **Carrier:** fragmentation mines and drone reinforcements. **Lattice:** volleys with open corridors. **Koschei:** inward-curving pincers released from black holes. These attacks develop through guardian phases.

## Weapons and pickups

- The HUD tracks earned weapon power from **0 to 12**, alongside shot count and blast strength.
- The first seven upgrades increase a volley from one shot to **eight shots**. Upgrades 8–12 each add 25% of base blast damage, reaching **2.25× blast strength** at maximum power.
- Permanent firing arrays begin in **wave three**, with one per remaining non-guardian wave: **5–7 arrays per level**. Weapon power survives weapon changes and resets at the next level.
- A rare gold-star **Supercharge** grants maximum weapon power for **six seconds**. Each level has a 30% chance to place one in an eligible later combat wave. Earned upgrades remain separate and return when the boost expires; pausing freezes its timer.

## Settings and reliability

- **Training Wheels** in Settings enables enemy return traces, asteroid routes, predicted shot paths, impact zones and safe-lane guides. It starts **off** for fresh and older saves. Real enemies, projectiles, mines and black-hole portals remain visible in either mode.
- The option saves on the current device and can be changed during pause without restarting the flight. Reduced Motion remains a separate setting.
- Existing scores, ship parts, tuning, transmissions and valid checkpoints are preserved. Saves that completed the original five-level campaign can continue into level six; story completion now retains an endless checkpoint.
- Offline updates recover more reliably from stale cached files. After one complete online load, the game can launch offline. Refresh to load an available update; active flights are not automatically reloaded.

## Save scope and private beta

Player progress is saved locally in the current browser and device, with checkpoints between levels. Mid-level progress and cross-device cloud saves are not included in this release.

Invited beta testers have a separate private feedback form, report inbox and owner email notifications. Those tools are available in private staging only. Beta access sign-in does not create a public game account or synchronize player progress.
